# Pamwe support

_Last updated: 3 August 2026_

Pamwe is a devotional space for two. You and your partner read the same passage, reflect on it separately, and what you each wrote stays sealed until you have both finished.

## Get help

Email **christianmangwanda@gmail.com**. Include what happened, what you expected, and your device model, and you will hear back within a few days.

## Common questions

**My partner's invite code will not work.**
Invite codes expire after 7 days. Ask your partner to open Pamwe and share a fresh code from their screen.

**The sign-in email did not arrive.**
Check your spam folder first. You can request a new link after a minute; the newest link is the one that works.

**We are behind on our plan. Did we lose everything?**
No. Days you missed stay open until you catch up, and readings you seal while catching up still count. You can also slow the rhythm to every other day or weekly under You, then Notifications and reminders.

**How do I delete my account?**
Inside the app: You, then Settings, then Delete account. It takes effect immediately and removes your reflections, voice recordings, prayers, and dreams. Your partner keeps everything they wrote. The [privacy policy](./) has the full list.

**Who can see what I write?**
Only your paired partner, and only after you have both submitted for that day. Nothing you write is public. The [privacy policy](./) covers everything Pamwe stores, who handles it, and how to erase it.
