Notes for the port

  • Colors are roles only: veil and ground are background, orb fill is sel, orb border and rings are accent-soft, initials and "Amen" are accent, glow is a pre-rendered soft asset tinted accent-soft in light and accent in dark.
  • The two press segments are one keyframe with a curve change at 53 percent. In Reanimated, run withSequence of two withTiming calls, 380ms accelerating then 340ms on the settle curve.
  • The travel and the press are separate animated values on nested views so neither fights for the same transform.
  • Glow holds at 0.3 opacity after its flare and only leaves with the veil, so the meeting point stays warm while the flower grows.
  • Vines scale on X from the edge nearest the bloom, so the embroidery reads as growing outward rather than sliding in.
  • 60fps check: at most nine animated layers are live at once and none of them change layout, only transform and opacity.
